# How to Connect ListingIQ with Hospitable

ListingIQ gives you an instant AI-powered optimization report for every property in your Hospitable account — no URL pasting required.

## Connection Steps

1. Go to **[listingiq.pro/hospitable](https://listingiq.pro/hospitable)**
2. Click **"Connect Hospitable"**
3. Log in to your Hospitable account and authorize ListingIQ (read-only access — we never modify your data)
4. Your properties will load automatically
5. Pick a property and choose your plan:
   - **Quick Score ($29)** — title, description, amenities, SEO keywords, action plan
   - **Full Audit ($49)** — everything above + AI photo analysis with gallery reorder
6. Complete payment
7. Your scored optimization report is delivered instantly on screen

## What ListingIQ Reads

- Property details (title, description, amenities, photos, location)
- Connected listing info (Airbnb listing URL)
- Reviews (for sentiment analysis)

## What ListingIQ Does NOT Access

- No calendar, reservation, or pricing data
- No guest personal information
- ListingIQ never writes to your Hospitable account

## After Your Report

- View your report on screen immediately
- Export as PDF or email it for later reference
- Re-analyze anytime after updating your listing
- Access previous reports from the History tab

## Disconnecting

To disconnect ListingIQ from your Hospitable account, click **"Disconnect"** on the properties page. This removes your connection and deletes stored tokens.
